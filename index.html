JobFlow

CV → matched jobs → you apply.

1. Your CV

Upload your PDF/DOCX/TXT. JobFlow analyses it in your browser and builds a profile for matching.

2. What you want

Hard filters are built in: internships, obvious call-centre/customer-service roles, and roles explicitly requiring 3+ years or a bachelor's/master's are heavily penalised or excluded.

3. Your matches

Upload your CV and tap Find matching jobs.
